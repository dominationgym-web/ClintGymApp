## 2. What we collect

- **Account details** you give us at signup: name, email, phone number.
- **Coaching information** your trainer records: goals, injuries, and any
  intake-form answers you provide about your routine and habits.
- **Daily check-in data** you submit: alcohol intake, sleep times and
  quality, water intake, meals and high-GI food timing, screen time before
  bed, reading and breathing/stretching habits, and any distress or pain
  you choose to flag, with your note.
- **Status updates** you set yourself (urgent / feedback wanted / all
  good), with an optional note.
- **Training videos** you upload as proof of a session.
- **Plan and payment status**: which plan you're on, whether your account
  is active, and your plan dates. We do **not** collect or store your card
  or bank login details anywhere in the app — payment happens directly by
  EFT or PayPal outside the app, and your trainer manually marks your
  account active once they've confirmed it arrived.
- **Private trainer notes** your trainer makes about you. These are not
  shared back to you inside the app.

We do not collect your password directly - it's handled entirely by our
authentication provider and is never visible to your trainer.

## 3. Why we collect it

Solely to provide the coaching service you signed up for: tracking your
daily accountability, understanding your training and lifestyle so your
program can be built around it, reviewing your check-ins and flags so your
trainer can respond appropriately, and confirming your payment/access
status.

## 4. Is this voluntary?

Providing your name, email, and consent is required to create an account.
Everything else - check-ins, videos, intake answers, status flags - is
provided by you as part of using the coaching service; if you don't
provide it, your trainer simply has less information to coach you with.
You can decline to answer any specific question without losing access to
your account.

## 5. Special personal information (health-related data)

Some of what you share - sleep, alcohol use, and any distress, pain, or
health-related notes - is "special personal information" under POPIA
Section 26, which is otherwise prohibited from being processed. We only
process it because you give **explicit, informed consent** at signup,
which POPIA Section 27 allows. You can withdraw this consent at any time
by contacting us - though we may no longer be able to coach you
effectively without it, and withdrawing consent doesn't affect information
already legitimately collected.

## 6. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored with our database and hosting provider,
Supabase, on servers located in Ireland (European Union). This means your
personal information leaves South Africa. The EU maintains a data
protection framework (GDPR) that is broadly regarded as offering an
adequate level of protection. Supabase acts only as our data processor -
they don't use your information for their own purposes.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Training videos** are automatically deleted 30 days after upload.
- **Everything else** is kept for as long as you're an active client.
  If you close your account or ask us to delete your information, we will
  do so within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep
  limited records for legal or accounting reasons (e.g. payment history).

## 8. Who else sees your information

- **Your trainer** - that's the point of the app.
- **Supabase**, as our data processor, under a standard processing
  arrangement - not for their own purposes.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell your information, and we do not share it
  with advertisers or other third parties for marketing.

## 9. No automated decision-making

Nothing in this app makes automated decisions about you. Your trainer
reviews your check-ins, flags, and videos personally.

## 10. Security

We rely on our provider's encryption in transit and at rest, and
access-control rules that mean other clients can never see your data,
videos, or check-ins - only you and your trainer can.

## 11. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:
- Ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy
- Ask us to correct information that's wrong or out of date
- Object to how we're processing your information
- Ask us to delete your information (subject to section 7 above)
- Withdraw your consent at any time
- Complain to the Information Regulator if you believe we've handled your
  information incorrectly:
  - Email: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za
  - Website: https://inforegulator.org.za

## 12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the version
date above and let you know. Continuing to use the app after a change
means you accept the updated policy.
